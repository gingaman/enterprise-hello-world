# HelloWorld™ 2.1: Enterprise Salutation Transformation Programme

**Classification:** Satirical proposal, not an actual roadmap or funded programme  
**Programme sponsor:** Fictional Group Executive, Enterprise Greeting Experience  
**Proposed start:** 9 November 2026  
**Proposed delivery:** After 24 months of discovery, subject to re-baselining  
**Business outcome:** Continue emitting `Hello, World!`

## Executive proposition

HelloWorld™ has successfully established a complex way to produce a constant string.
Phase 2.1 will complete the transformation by ensuring the string has a commercial
model, an AI strategy, a disaster-recovery plan, a zero-trust model, an employee
survey and a replacement platform proposal.

> The target business capability is unchanged. The target organisational capability is unlimited.

## Delivery and decision rights

1. **Months 0–6:** commission an independent definition of the word 'Hello'.
2. **Months 7–12:** agree the operating model for the comma.
3. **Months 13–18:** launch governance and cost attribution pilots; no greeting changes.
4. **Months 19–24:** prepare the migration business case and recommend further discovery.
5. **Beyond Month 24:** reassess whether `print("Hello, World!")` remains fit for purpose.

No change to runtime behaviour, Kubernetes manifests, telemetry or customer-facing services is proposed in this documentation-only increment.

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Response |
|---|---|---|
| A developer prints the string directly | High | Refer to Architecture Review Board |
| Another team discovers the standard library | High | Classify as shadow IT |
| The transformation finishes early | Low | Commission a re-baselining exercise |
| Committee decisions are unanimous | Medium | Create a subcommittee to assess groupthink |
| A stakeholder asks for the business outcome | Certain | Refer them to the mission statement |

**Final board proposition:** A simple solution is an enterprise risk until documented otherwise.
