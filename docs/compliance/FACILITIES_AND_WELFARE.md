# Facilities and Welfare Compliance Statement

**Policy ID:** FAC-001
**Status:** Under consultation (week 14 of an expected 6)
**Owner:** To be confirmed. See §4.
**Scope:** All contributors, maintainers, reviewers and the CI pipeline

> This is satire. HelloWorld™ is a software repository and contains no
> plumbing. That fact has not stopped this document from being written.

## 1. Purpose

The Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992 require employers
to provide suitable sanitary conveniences (regulation 20) and washing
facilities (regulation 21). An audit question asked whether HelloWorld™
complies. Nobody could say no with sufficient confidence, so this statement
exists.

## 2. Current provision

| Facility | Provided | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Sanitary conveniences | 0 | — |
| Washing facilities | 0 | — |
| Drinking water | 0 | — |
| Rest area | 1 | `#platform-helloworld` Slack channel |
| Fire assembly point | 1 | The `main` branch |

The Kubernetes manifest sets `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true`, which prevents the
installation of fixtures inside the container. Security has declined to grant
an exception for a cistern.

## 3. Facilities Impact Assessment

A Facilities Impact Assessment (FIA) found that:

- all contributors work remotely, on their own premises, using their own conveniences;
- the repository's welfare obligations therefore fall to each contributor's house;
- the CI runner has no recorded welfare requirements, but was not asked.

The FIA recommends no action. That recommendation is now subject to review by
the GCAB, because a toilet sign may contain punctuation.

## 4. Ownership of the toilet policy

The question of what kind of toilet provision the repository should have was
referred to five bodies at once. Each has confirmed in writing that the
decision belongs to another.

| Body | Position |
|---|---|
| Estates | A matter for HR, as it concerns people |
| HR | A matter for Legal, as it concerns law |
| Legal | Silent. Silence is not to be construed as approval unless useful. |
| GCAB | Out of scope, unless signage punctuation is affected |
| Architecture Review Board | Requires a 30-slide deck before forming a view |

The repository currently has no toilets of any kind. This position is fully
compliant with every policy option under consideration and has therefore been
adopted as the interim operating model.

## 6. Statement

HelloWorld™ is committed to the welfare of everyone who contributes to it. It
expresses that commitment in documents rather than buildings, which is the
industry standard.
