# Zero Trust Punctuation™

**Programme:** HelloWorld™ 2.1 Enterprise Salutation Transformation  
**Workstream:** ES-02  
**Owner (fictional):** Chief Character Security Officer  
**Status:** Proposed; discovery funding not approved

> **Satire:** this proposal is fictional. Nothing in this document asserts that the described services, expenditures, measurements or offices exist.

## Strategic objective

Assume every punctuation mark is hostile until authenticated, authorised and logged.

## Proposed target architecture and operating model

1. Introduce a Punctuation Identity Provider to issue short-lived identities to commas and exclamation marks.
2. Require mutual TLS between the Greeting Orchestrator and a proposed Character Entitlement Service.
3. Apply least-privilege policy: a comma may separate clauses but may not impersonate an exclamation mark.
4. Rotate punctuation credentials every 15 minutes, including when the message is not running.
5. Classify unauthorised enthusiasm as a high-priority security event.

## Executive success measures

- Mean time to revoke an exclamation mark: less than 15 minutes.
- Punctuation identity coverage: 100%, excluding legacy whitespace.
- Zero unapproved uppercase enthusiasm events.

## Governance decision requested

Approve discovery of a machine identity programme for the 13-character greeting.

## Programme observation

> Security has confirmed that the word 'Hello' is not inherently trusted merely because it appears at the start of the message.
