# Greeting-as-a-Service (GaaS)

**Programme:** HelloWorld™ 2.1 Enterprise Salutation Transformation  
**Workstream:** ES-05  
**Owner (fictional):** Vice-President, Salutation Monetisation  
**Status:** Proposed; discovery funding not approved

> **Satire:** this proposal is fictional. Nothing in this document asserts that the described services, expenditures, measurements or offices exist.

## Strategic objective

Turn a terminal output line into a repeatable subscription revenue stream.

## Proposed target architecture and operating model

1. Community tier provides 'Hello' with no world entitlement.
2. Enterprise tier unlocks 'World' and geographically redundant commas.
3. Premium tier unlocks the exclamation mark, prioritised punctuation support and dedicated account management.
4. Introduce a usage-based billing engine that counts a greeting when emitted and again when acknowledged.
5. Reserve annual price rises for customers who have not yet been identified.

## Executive success measures

- Monthly recurring salutation revenue: £0, pending customer acquisition.
- Average revenue per punctuation mark.
- Gross retention: 100%, on a customer base of zero.

## Governance decision requested

Authorise a pricing study and an enterprise sales pack before creating an endpoint customers can call.

## Programme observation

> The free tier is functionally incomplete by design. Product regards this as an upsell opportunity.

## Integration and dependencies

This workstream must align with the [programme portfolio](../strategy/HELLOWORLD_2_1_TRANSFORMATION.md), the existing [Greeting Change Advisory Board](../governance/GREETING_CHANGE_ADVISORY_BOARD.md) and the [fictional executive dashboard](../dashboard/EXECUTIVE_DASHBOARD.md). No application changes are authorised by this document.

**Acceptance criterion:** The initiative must be demonstrably more complicated than printing `Hello, World!`, while delivering exactly the same greeting.
