# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

**Programme:** HelloWorld™ 2.1 Enterprise Salutation Transformation  
**Workstream:** ES-04  
**Owner (fictional):** Head of Resilient Salutation Services  
**Status:** Proposed; discovery funding not approved

> **Satire:** this proposal is fictional. Nothing in this document asserts that the described services, expenditures, measurements or offices exist.

## Strategic objective

Maintain greeting continuity even when the primary greeting encounters an existential punctuation problem.

## Proposed target architecture and operating model

1. Designate 'Hi, World!' as the warm disaster-recovery greeting, subject to separate branding approval.
2. Maintain an alternative greeting in a second failure domain, whose independence is to be verified by an external consultancy.
3. Trigger failover only after a declared disaster, an executive decision and an authorised tabletop exercise.
4. Require restoration of 'Hello, World!' within a Recovery Time Objective of four quarters.
5. Classify the loss of 'World' as a more severe event than the loss of the exclamation mark.

## Executive success measures

- Recovery Time Objective: four business quarters.
- Recovery Point Objective: zero previously approved greetings.
- Tabletop exercise pass rate: 100% among participants who attended.

## Governance decision requested

Procure a secondary greeting before anyone determines whether the primary one is stateful.

## Programme observation

> The fallback is nine characters shorter in the business case because the project counts the spaces occupied by the documentation.
