# Employee Greeting Experience Survey

**Programme:** HelloWorld™ 2.1 Enterprise Salutation Transformation  
**Workstream:** ES-07  
**Owner (fictional):** Director of Organisational Salutation Culture  
**Status:** Proposed; discovery funding not approved

> **Satire:** this proposal is fictional. Nothing in this document asserts that the described services, expenditures, measurements or offices exist.

## Strategic objective

Demonstrate exceptional employee and customer engagement through quarterly feedback.

## Proposed target architecture and operating model

1. Survey users of HelloWorld™ every quarter using a form circulated to an empty distribution list.
2. Treat a missing negative response as a favourable sentiment indicator.
3. Publish 100% satisfaction with an explicit sample size of zero; do not infer that anybody responded.
4. Create a 'You Said, We Listened' programme for concerns that nobody submitted.
5. Offer a listening session, provided it is attended by representatives from every stakeholder group.

## Executive success measures

- Positive response proportion: not computable, because n=0.
- Executive dashboard satisfaction headline: 100% (fictional management interpretation).
- Survey completion rate: undefined, subject to narrative smoothing.

## Governance decision requested

Approve a recognition award for exceeding the stakeholder engagement target.

## Programme observation

> The survey has achieved the best outcome ever recorded: nobody complained.
