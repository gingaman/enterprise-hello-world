# Greeting FinOps™

**Programme:** HelloWorld™ 2.1 Enterprise Salutation Transformation  
**Workstream:** ES-01  
**Owner (fictional):** Head of Unit Economics and Punctuation  
**Status:** Proposed; discovery funding not approved

> **Satire:** this proposal is fictional. Nothing in this document asserts that the described services, expenditures, measurements or offices exist.

## Strategic objective

Reduce the cost of each greeting by 30% against a baseline that the organisation has not yet measured.

## Proposed target architecture and operating model

1. Stand up a Greeting Cost Allocation Engine (GCAE) alongside the existing stdout emitter.
2. Allocate Kubernetes, legal review, committee catering, observability and consultancy charges to each successful salutation.
3. Showback every character to its notional cost centre. Chargeback is blocked pending creation of the cost centres.
4. Use an annualised total cost of ownership model for a program whose active work is shorter than its business-case approval process.

## Executive success measures

- Cost per successfully greeted world, in GBP; baseline: TBD.
- Unit-cost reduction target: 30% without a denominator.
- Percentage of greetings with full cost attribution: 100% in the PowerPoint model.

## Governance decision requested

Approve a six-month assessment to determine what a greeting costs before approving any expenditure to reduce it.
